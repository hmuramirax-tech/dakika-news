## 1. Store Listing Content

### App Name
**OneNews**

### Short Description (80 chars)
```
AI news summaries in 60 seconds. Read the day in Kinyarwanda, English & Swahili.
```

### Full Description (4000 chars)
```
OneNews is a mobile-first AI news application that distills East African news into 60-second reads, designed for busy professionals who want to stay informed without spending hours scrolling.

KEY FEATURES:

🔹 60-Second AI Summaries
Get concise 40-80 word summaries of the day's top stories, generated by AI and reviewed by human editors.

🔹 Local Languages
Available in Kinyarwanda, English, and Swahili from day one.

🔹 Offline-First Design
Download digests and read during commutes, even with no signal. Cache up to 3 digests (30 stories).

🔹 Mobile Money Payments
Subscribe for only Rwf 300/month (~$0.20) via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or M-Pesa.

🔹 Source Attribution
Every summary clearly shows its original source. Trust through transparency.

🔹 Category Feeds
Sports, Business, Tech, Politics, Entertainment, Health — follow what matters to you.

🔹 Search
Search past digests by keyword, category, or date.

🔹 SMS News Service
Send "NEWS" to [shortcode] for daily SMS digests (feature phone friendly).

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Phone + OTP authentication (no passwords)
• All data encrypted in transit and at rest
• Compliant with Rwanda Data Protection Law (2021)
• No sale of personal data to third parties

ABOUT US
OneNews is built by a small team of passionate technologists and journalists based in Kigali, Rwanda. We believe everyone deserves access to quality news in their own language.

CONTACT
Email: hello@onenews.rw
Website: https://onenews.rw
```
