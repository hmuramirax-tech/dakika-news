## 4. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Play Store rejection | Medium | High | Submit early, have backup plan |
| Server overload | Low | High | Auto-scaling, CDN |
| Negative press | Medium | Medium | Prepared responses, quick action |
| Low downloads | Medium | High | Paid ads, influencer outreach |
| Critical bug | Low | High | Hotfix process, rollback plan |
